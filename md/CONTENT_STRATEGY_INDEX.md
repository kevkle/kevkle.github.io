### 4. BLOG_POST_TEMPLATES.md
**Length:** 10 pages | **Read Time:** 15-20 minutes

**What you get:**
- 4 reusable blog post templates
- Step-by-step structure for each
- Writing tips to write faster
- Publishing checklist
- Post-publishing promotion strategy
- First blog post recommendation

**Best for:** Writing your first 3-5 articles without starting from scratch

**4 Templates:**

1. **Technical Deep-Dive (2,500-3,000 words)**
   - Hook → Problem → Approach → Lessons → Results → Frameworks → CTA
   - Example: "Designing Agentic AI Systems for Regulated Healthcare"

2. **Lessons Learned / Retrospective (1,500-2,000 words)**
   - Context → Mistake 1 → Mistake 2 → Mistake 3 → Meta-lesson → CTA
   - Example: "5 Mistakes I Made Building Agentic AI (and What I Learned)"

3. **Tutorial / How-To (1,000-1,500 words)**
   - Intro → Prerequisites → Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Troubleshooting → CTA
   - Example: "How to Set Up Low-Latency LLM Inference on Kubernetes"

4. **Industry Insight (800-1,200 words)**
   - Hook → Trend → Why It Matters → What's Next → CTA
   - Example: "Why Healthcare Is Finally Ready for Agentic AI"

**Bonus:**
- Writing faster tips (voice-to-text, write messy first, time-box)
- First post recommendation: "Agentic AI in Regulated Healthcare"
- Content repurposing ideas (blog → LinkedIn → Twitter thread → talk)

## FAQ: Using This Strategy

**Q: Where should I start?**
A: Read CONTENT_REVIEW_SUMMARY.md (5 min), then Phase 1 of CONTENT_ACTION_PLAN.md. Execute Phase 1 first (4-5 hours, high ROI).

**Q: How much time will this take?**
A: Phase 1: 4-5 hours (quick wins). Article writing: 4-5 hours each. Total for 3 months: 40-60 hours. That's 9-12 hours/week, very manageable alongside your day job.

**Q: Should I do this instead of looking for a job?**
A: No. This is something to do in parallel. It takes 2-3 hours/week. The ROI is that you'll get 3-5x more inbound inquiries while your job search runs.

**Q: What if I don't want to write blog posts?**
A: Do Phases 1, 3, and 4 only (testimonials, CTAs, optimization). That's still 2x improvement. But blog posts are the force multiplier—they compound over time.

**Q: Can I repurpose existing work into articles?**
A: Absolutely. Every project case study on your portfolio can become a blog post. Your 4 projects = at least 4 articles ready to write.

**Q: Should I focus on LinkedIn or a personal blog?**
A: Both. Publish to LinkedIn first (reach hiring managers directly). Also publish to Medium/personal blog (SEO benefit, owned channel). Takes extra 30 min per article.

**Q: What if I get an offer while in the middle of this?**
A: Great! The strategy is designed to be flexible. Do Phase 1 (quick wins) immediately. If you get an offer, you're done. If not, continue with Phase 2 (content).

**Q: How do I measure if this is working?**
A: See CONTENT_ACTION_PLAN.md Phase 4 (Measurement & Optimization). Track: visitors, CTA clicks, email signups, speaking inquiries.

   - [ ] Build advisory/consulting pipeline
   - [ ] Establish thought leadership
   - [ ] All of the above

2. **How much time can I realistically commit?**
   - [ ] 2-3 hours/week (slow and steady)
   - [ ] 5-10 hours/week (good pace)
   - [ ] 15+ hours/week (aggressive)

3. **What's my biggest priority?**

   - [ ] CTA optimization (quick win)

   - [ ] Medium account
