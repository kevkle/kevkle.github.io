## Messaging for Different Content Types

### Blog Articles
**Structure:**
1. Open with specific problem (not generic)
2. Explain why naive approaches fail
3. Walk through your approach + trade-offs
4. Show results or lessons learned
5. Close with: implications for the reader

**Tone:** Educational, honest about mistakes/trade-offs

**Example Hook:**
> "Most AI systems are built for speed. Healthcare demands something different: auditability. Here's how I designed one system to meet that constraint."

---

### Case Studies / Project Deep-Dives
**Structure:**
1. Problem (business + technical)
2. Constraints (regulations, latency, accuracy trade-offs)
3. Your approach (why this over alternatives)
4. Implementation (specific patterns, decisions)
5. Results + lessons

**Tone:** Detailed, humble about what worked and what didn't

**Example Framing:**
> "We tried three approaches. Here's why two failed and what worked instead."

---

### Speaking / Podcasts
**Structure:**
1. Interesting problem from your experience
2. Most engineers would do X; I did Y. Here's why.
3. Unexpected lesson or insight
4. Actionable takeaway for audience

**Tone:** Conversational, confident but not cocky

**Example Opener:**
> "I once spent a week optimizing model accuracy only to discover the real problem was a latency bottleneck in serving. Here's what I learned about healthcare ML systems..."

---

### LinkedIn Posts
**Structure:**
- Hook (personal insight or interesting pattern)
- Context (why this matters)
- Actionable insight or lesson
- Call-to-action (comment with your experience)

**Tone:** Authentic, thoughtful, accessible

**Example:**
> "Just realized: 'production ML' means different things to different domains. Finance wants low latency. Healthcare wants auditability. This distinction changed how I design systems. What's your domain's #1 constraint?"
