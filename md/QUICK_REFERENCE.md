## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't write generic content ("10 ML tips everyone knows")
❌ Don't claim expertise you don't have
❌ Don't focus on tools instead of principles
❌ Don't skip the testimonials ("I'll do it later" = never)
❌ Don't write one article and stop
❌ Don't spend 100 hours perfecting one piece
❌ Don't hide your work in PDF downloads (blog = SEO juice)
